# Privacy Policy for Crate

**Last updated: July 15, 2026**

Black Box Audio, LLC ("Black Box Audio," "we," "us," or "our") develops and operates
Crate, a DJ music library and discovery application available on iOS, Android, and
desktop (collectively, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App
handles, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have.

## The short version

Crate is local-first and privacy-first:

- The App works fully offline. By default, your data stays on your device.
- Cloud sync is **optional and off by default**. It only turns on if you choose to sign
  in and enable it.
- We do **not** use any advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking, or crash-reporting
  services. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share
  your personal information.
- **Your audio files never leave your device.**

The rest of this policy provides the detail required by the app stores and by privacy
laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

## 2. Scope

## 3. Information we collect

How much information the App handles depends entirely on whether you enable optional cloud
sync.

### 3.1 If you do NOT enable cloud sync (default)

If you never sign in, the App does not send any personal information to us. Everything you
create — your saved releases, playlists, tags, notes, and settings — is stored only in a
local, encrypted database on your device. We have no account for you and cannot access
this data.

The only network connections the App makes in this mode are to the third-party music
services you choose to browse or preview (see Section 6), and, on desktop only, to check
for app updates and load web fonts.

### 3.2 If you enable cloud sync (optional)

Cloud sync lets you keep your Crate data in sync across your devices. To use it, you sign
in with Google and turn sync on. When you do, we (through our cloud provider, Google
Firebase) collect and store:

**a) Account information.** When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your basic
Google profile:

- Email address
- Display name
- Profile photo (a link to your Google profile picture)
- A unique account identifier (Firebase user ID)

We never see or receive your Google password. Sign-in is handled by Google.

**b) Content you sync.** The content you have chosen to keep in Crate, so it can appear on
your other devices. On mobile, this is your discovery and organization data:

- Saved releases and their track details (titles, artists, durations, artwork links, and
  the source URLs you added)
- Playlists and playlist contents
- Tags and tag categories
- Notes you write on releases
- Artists and labels you follow (including the URLs you added)
- A small set of app preferences (theme, accent color, language, date format,
  key-notation format, and the "auto-analyze on import" toggle)

On the desktop app, synced content additionally includes metadata about tracks in your
local library (such as titles, artists, ratings, play counts, and the file paths and
content hashes that let the desktop app locate your files).

**Your audio files are never uploaded.** Only text metadata is synced. Album and track
artwork images are not uploaded either; your other devices re-fetch artwork from its
original source.

**c) Device information.** To manage which devices are connected to your account, we
store, for each device you sync: a device identifier generated by the App, the device
name, the app version, and the time it last synced. This lets you see and revoke your
devices.

**d) Technical connection data.** Like any internet service, when the App communicates
with our cloud provider, Google's servers automatically process technical data such as
your device's IP address and the time of the request in order to route and secure the
connection. We do not use this to build a profile of you or to track you.

### 3.3 App integrity check (mobile)

On mobile, to protect the cloud service from abuse, the App uses Google Firebase App Check
together with your platform's built-in attestation (Apple App Attest on iOS, Google Play
Integrity on Android). This confirms that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of
the App on a genuine device. It does not read your personal data and does not give us a
persistent identifier that tracks you.

### 3.4 What we do NOT collect

We want to be explicit. The App does **not**:

- Contain any advertising or advertising identifiers (no IDFA, no Google Advertising ID).
- Include any analytics, telemetry, usage-tracking, or crash-reporting service. Diagnostic
  information (such as app logs) stays on your device and is only shown to you; it is never
  transmitted to us.
- Collect your location.
- Access your contacts, photo library, microphone, or camera.
- Track you across other apps or websites. (On iOS, the App does not use App Tracking
  Transparency because it performs no tracking.)

## 4. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

- Provide and operate optional cloud sync, so your Crate data appears across your devices.
- Authenticate you and secure your account.
- Manage your connected devices and let you revoke them.
- Protect the service against fraud and abuse (app integrity checks).
- Respond to your support requests and communicate with you about the service.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making
that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do **not** sell or "share" (as
defined under California law) your personal information.

## 5. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following
legal bases under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the cloud sync and account
  features you request.
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the service secure and prevent abuse
  (e.g., app integrity checks), balanced against your rights.
- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where required. You provide consent by choosing to enable
  cloud sync; you can withdraw it at any time by turning off sync and/or deleting your
  account.
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we must process data to comply with the law.

## 6. Third-party services

### 6.1 Cloud infrastructure (only if you enable sync)

We use **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** (provided by Google LLC) as our cloud provider
for authentication, database, file storage, and app-integrity checks. When you enable
sync, your account and synced content are stored on Google's infrastructure on our behalf.
Google processes this data as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy at
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> and Firebase's Privacy and Security information at
<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>.

### 6.2 Music discovery sources

Crate's discovery features let you view and preview music hosted by third parties. When
you open, browse, or preview a release from one of these sources, the App requests that
content directly from the relevant service, which necessarily reveals your device's IP
address to them. The App does not send them your Crate account information. These services
include:

- **Bandcamp** — <https://bandcamp.com/privacy>
- **SoundCloud** — <https://soundcloud.com/pages/privacy>
- **YouTube / Google** — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **Discogs** — <https://www.discogs.com/legal>

Your use of content from these services is subject to their terms and privacy policies.

### 6.3 App distribution

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. When you download or
update the App, Apple and Google process information under their own privacy policies.

We are based in the United States, and our cloud provider (Google) stores data on

processed in the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by

## 8. Data retention

- **Local data** remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- **Synced data** (account and content) is retained for as long as your account exists so
  we can provide the service.
- When you delete your account (see Section 10), we delete your synced content and account
  record. Backups and logs, if any, are purged within a commercially reasonable period
  (no more than 30 days).

## 9. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

- On your device, your library is stored in an **encrypted (SQLCipher) database**. On iOS
  and Android, the encryption key is held in the platform's secure storage (Keychain /
  Android Keystore) and is not backed up to the cloud.
- Data in transit between the App and our cloud provider is protected with **HTTPS/TLS**.
- Synced data stored with Google is protected by Google's server-side encryption.

Please note that some on-device caches (such as cached preview audio and artwork) and any
backup files you create are stored unencrypted on your device; protecting your device with
a passcode or biometrics helps keep them safe. No method of transmission or storage is
100% secure, but we work to protect your information using accepted practices.

  <https://crate.bbx-audio.com/account-deletion>.

personal information we hold:

- **Access** a copy of your information.
- **Correct** inaccurate information.

- **Port** your information to another service.

- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent.
