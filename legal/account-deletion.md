# Delete Your Crate Account

**Last updated: July 15, 2026**

Crate is local-first, and cloud sync (which requires an account) is optional. If you never
enabled cloud sync, you do not have an account with us, and there is nothing to delete on
our side — simply uninstalling the App removes your local data.

If you signed in and used cloud sync, here is how to delete your account and all associated
data.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open Crate.
2. Go to **Settings → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and all synced data from our servers.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the App, email **matthew@bbx-audio.com** from the email address
associated with your account (or tell us the email you used to sign in) with the subject
line **"Delete my account."** We will verify your request and delete your account.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following from our cloud service:

- Your account record (email address, name, profile photo link, and account identifier).
- All synced content (saved releases, playlists, tags, notes, followed artists and labels,
  and synced settings).
- Your device records.

Backups and server logs, if any, are purged within 30 days.

Data stored locally on your own devices is not affected by server-side deletion. To remove
it, sign out and/or uninstall the App from each device.

## Timing

In-app deletions take effect immediately. Email requests are completed within 30 days
(typically much sooner).
